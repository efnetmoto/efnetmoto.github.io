# EFNet #motorcycles

We've been on EFNet as #motorcycles since 1998, a conversation channel for people
who ride, wrench, race, and otherwise care about motorcycles. You don't need to
own a bike to join us, but it can be a side effect.

We're a small channel, somewhere around 15 to 25 regulars, and most of us have
been here for the better part of its life. The talk runs the whole range: track
days and tire temps, touring and moto-camping, vintage, dirt, Harleys, modern
hypersports, wrenching, gear, and bike reviews. After knowing each other this
long, it's at least as much off-topic life stuff as it is bikes. It's the coffee
stop on a ride, over text.

We're mostly US but genuinely global. Over the years enough of us have become
actual friends that a couple of rides happen: an annual get-together at Deals
Gap that goes back to '99 or '00, and GapWest, a semiannual NorCal meetup we
started in 2022.

# Deals Gap

Since the turn of the century, a bunch of us from the channel meet up at Deals
Gap the first week of May every year — the stretch of US-129 on the
Tennessee/North Carolina line most people call the Tail of the Dragon — to
ride, hang out, and carry on in person instead of in channel. The dates float
with the calendar, but the week doesn't.

We've always stayed at [The Treetops](https://thetreetops.com/) — same spot
every year, and worth reserving early once the dates are set.

To register or for more info, see the sign-up site at
[emcgap.us](https://www.emcgap.us/).
